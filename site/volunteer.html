Volunteer

Two hours a week makes a real difference.

Volunteers walk, feed, clean, cuddle and drive. Start with a one-hour orientation, then pick the shifts that suit you.

  • Dog walker

    Mornings · 2 hours

    Walks before the heat, and play time in the yard. Ages 16 and up.

  • Cat socializer

    Any time we're open · 1–2 hours

    Gentle play and handling so shy cats learn to trust people.

  • Adoption-day helper

    Saturdays · 4 hours

    Set up, answer questions and help animals meet their new families.

  • Driver

    On call

    Take animals to vet visits and bring rescues in from partner shelters.

  • Skills volunteer

    Flexible

    Photography, grant writing, carpentry, social media: tell us what you're good at.
