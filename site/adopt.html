How adoption works

Four steps, usually about a week

  1. Ask about an animal

    Send a short enquiry. We'll reply within two days.

  2. Meet and greet

    Visit at the rescue, with your family and any dog you have.

  3. Home check

    A friendly video call to see where your new friend will live.

  4. Go home together

    Sign the adoption agreement, pay the fee and take home a starter kit.
