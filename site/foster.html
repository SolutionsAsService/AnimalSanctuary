Foster

Lend us your home for a few weeks.

Every foster home frees a kennel for the next animal in need. Kittens, recovering dogs and shy animals all do better in a real home while they wait.

What we provide

You give the time. We cover the rest.

  • Food, litter, bedding, crates and toys
  • All vet care, at our partner clinic
  • A foster coordinator you can text any time
  • Training help for anything tricky
  • First pick if you fall in love and want to adopt

Who we need

Foster homes we're always short of

  • Kitten season

    March to October, litters too young for adoption need two to six weeks of bottle or bowl feeding.

  • Recovering dogs

    Dogs healing after surgery need a quiet room and short walks for two to four weeks.

  • Shy animals

    Some animals need patience and a calm home before they're ready to meet adopters.
