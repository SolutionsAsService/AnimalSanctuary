About

A small rescue with a big network of homes.

We started in 2014 in a garage on Brown Road. Today a team of four, a partner veterinary clinic and nearly fifty foster homes care for every animal we take in.

How we work

Our promises to every animal

  1. No healthy or treatable animal is put down for space. When we're full, we find another foster home.
  2. Every animal sees a vet first. Vaccines, microchip and a full check before anything else.
  3. We take them back. If an adoption ever doesn't work out, the animal comes home to us, at any age.
