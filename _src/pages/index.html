How you can help

Four ways in, from an afternoon to a lifetime

  • Adopt

    Give a rescued animal a forever home.

  • Foster

    Care for an animal at home for two to eight weeks. We cover the costs.

  • Volunteer

    Walk dogs, socialize cats, help at adoption days.

  • Give

    Food, medicine and shelter, paid for by people like you.

Our mission

A world where every animal finds a loving home.

Through rescue, community outreach and adoption, we give every animal a second chance. We work hand in hand with veterinarians, foster families and volunteers, and we never put down a healthy or treatable animal for space.
