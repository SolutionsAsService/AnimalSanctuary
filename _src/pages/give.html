Give

What a gift pays for.

Most of what we spend goes on food and vet care. Here's what each amount covers, and the supplies we always need.

This is a concept website, so it takes no donations. On a live site, these amounts would link to the rescue's donation page.

  • $25

    A week of food for a dog in foster care

  • $60

    Vaccines and a microchip for one animal

  • $150

    A spay or neuter surgery

  • $500

    Emergency surgery for an injured rescue

Wish list

Drop off supplies any day we're open

  • Unopened dry and wet dog and cat food
  • Clumping cat litter
  • Timothy hay for rabbits
  • Towels and blankets (no feather stuffing)
  • Leashes, harnesses and sturdy chew toys
